# Instagram caption — GitHub promo reel (draft, 2026-09-06)

How I make these videos: it's all public now 🧑‍💻

Every reel on this page is built the same way: I record on my phone, then a pipeline
measures something real and burns it into the video. Vowel formants graded against
published references. Two independent pose trackers counting pull-ups. An EMG-informed
muscle map. Even this clip: the hand skeleton is MediaPipe's 21-point hand model, the
subtitles carry the IPA transcription, and the waves at the bottom are the actual
spectrum of my voice.

All the scripts, the reference data, the reports and the notes on what went wrong are at
github.com/DyeAllPies/dyeallpies-productions 📦 No recordings, no finished videos, just the
methods and the machine learning.
