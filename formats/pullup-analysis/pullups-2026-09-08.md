Dennis's brief: set #1 (the full-length one) passed 140k views on Instagram; do the next one
seriously — research first, everything downloaded and cited, more anatomy in the muscle
visualisation, more heat, more calculation, a jungle with high-definition monkeys, and a

## The clip

63.6 s iPhone 14 MOV, rotation tag 90 → 1080×1920, 30 fps, 1909 frames, **10-bit HLG**. Phone
on the floor 2.8 m from the doorway, looking up 6.5°, almost no roll (0.3°), yaw 5.8°. Feet in
frame; at the top of every rep the whole head vanishes behind the bar and the underside of the
lintel it is mounted under. A black cat sits in the bottom of the frame from 15 s. 11 reps,
no failed attempt, a 3.9 s dead hang before the first pull.

## Timeline of the session (≈5 h)

1. **Research first, in parallel.** Six background agents (two at a time), each writing one
   file into `references/pullup-science/` with abstracts, open-access PDFs and CSV tables:
   EMG and anatomy (22 new papers), biomechanics and velocity (17), norms and energy, thermal
   skin physiology and permissive anatomy assets (Gray's plates, OpenSim models), an ML
   tooling survey with licences, and Reel virality notes. Dennis added mid-way: download
   everything reusable. `DOWNLOADS.md` logs every file.
2. **Critical look at #1 and #2 frame by frame** before touching the new clip (`PLAN-3.md`
   §1): #2's body read as a blue mannequin, its 20 s cut threw away the thing that worked in
   #1 (watching the reps), its text was too small for a phone, and the HLG footage had been
   decoded flat.
3. **Tone-mapped master.** libplacebo (bt.2390, Vulkan) beats zscale hable/mobius by eye;
   the plain decode is grey. `pullup/work3/master.mp4`, NVENC cq 12.
4. **Geometry.** Vertical VP from 100 long edges, horizontal VP from 149 (bar, lintel, tiles,
   shelves); f = 1950 px from their orthogonality; H = K Rᵀ K⁻¹; a residual 0.72° rotation so
   the fitted bar is level (the lintel tilts identically, so the residual was the model, not
   the bar). The first attempt mirrored the image: the plane normal must point away from the
   camera. Measurement happens in the rectified plane; the render never does.
5. **Trackers on the master** (MediaPipe 292 s, YOLO 839 s, torso, matte 26 min at 1.2 fps).
6. **`analyze_pullups3.py`** from v2: landmarks through H, the bar fitted where it is
   (`bar_y=`), the lintel too, a phase walker from the *local* base (the deep passive hang
   had swallowed the whole dead hang into rep 1's concentric: 3.2 s), rep-1 velocity
   reference with the fastest rep beside it, 25 % / 50 % landmarks, RIR as a range.
7. **The chin, four times.** (a) "Head hidden behind the lintel ⇒ chin ≥ 13.5 cm above the
   bar" — wrong: the bar is mounted at the back edge of the lintel's underside, so the head
   is hidden by the bar and the underside together and the true bound is only the bar's
   underside sight line. Found by looking at a rep top at full resolution with the highlights
   stretched. (b) YOLO's nose confidence collapses on reps 1–8 and stays at 0.7–0.9 on 9–11
   with the head gone: hallucinated, not a witness. (c) The RVM silhouette shows the neck
   reaching the bar's underside on every top and nothing more. (d) The estimator that stands:
   the face-axis chin at the last frame the face is genuinely seen on the way up (eyes ≥ 4 cm
   under the bar's underside and nose confidence > 0.35) plus the shoulder rise after it.
   −1.7 to −5.0 cm in plane, +3.4 cm computed allowance for a chin 8 cm behind the bar,
   band ±3 cm: **at the bar, 11/11**, the same finding as set #2.
8. **Scale confirmed by physics.** Shoulder-to-bar at the dead hang = 62.7 cm against
   0.332 × 188 = 62.4 cm. The arm anchor (617 px/m) under-reads because the wrist joint sits
   8 cm below the bar; say so rather than averaging it in.
9. **Reference windows are not interchangeable.** The arms-down window (5.5–6.3 s) was
   still close to the camera (face 24 cm tall); the stature window is the reach under the
   bar (7.8–9.2 s); the shoulder-tilt reference is arms-down after the set (57–58.6 s); the
   ear-to-shoulder reference is the *dead hang* — the reach pose reads 27–30 cm and made
   every top look like a 10 cm shrug.
10. **Thermal v3** (`pullup_thermal3.py`): serratus anterior, upper trapezius, middle deltoid,
    triceps, forearm extensors; lat → trapezius shift at the top; core keyed off hip sway.
    36 kJ over 42 s; lats +1.38 °C.
11. **Atlas v3** (`pullup_atlas3.py`): fibre-direction fields (parallel or fan to the tendon),
    boundaries as grooves with an inner highlight, belly shading from the distance
    transform, irregular thin striations, the frame's own luminance deeper. Ironbow scale
    chosen over the blue one on a side-by-side. Preview on hang / mid / top before any render.
12. **Renderer v3** (`render_pullup_overlay3.py`): 1.12× push-in (the bar must stay under
    the Instagram top band: (365 − 150) × 1.12 = 241), readouts as pills beside the elbow and
    hips because the left panels hid the left arm, muscle names once each, a perspective grid
    mapped back through H⁻¹, the cat pasted as the judge, a three-number card. Fonts ≥ 34 px.
13. **Backdrop.** Commons search via curl (the system Python's certificate store is expired;
    thumbnails need `iiurlwidth`, hand-built thumb URLs return 400), 17 originals downloaded
    with full credits into `pullup/assets/commons/CREDITS.json`, rembg (isnet, alpha matting)
    cut-outs — the ones on branches failed and were dropped — placed on the Mata Atlântica
    plate's trunks by `build_plate3.py`.
14. **Chest flush** measured again (+27 units, r = 0.93 with time, wall flat) and
    **re-interpreted**: skin blood flow falls in the first minute of exercise, so it is not
    muscle heat at the skin. The standing baseline is invalid (different distance and white
    balance).
15. Dashboard, report page, REPORT-3, CAPTION-3, this file.

## Mistakes worth not repeating

- Patching scripts with `python - <<'EOF'` here mangles `\n` escapes and non-ASCII (the
  renderer got a cp1252 byte and would not import). Write patch scripts to a file with the
  Write tool, always `encoding="utf-8"`, and build multi-line strings with `chr(10)`.
- A background render piped through `grep` shows no progress for an hour. Run the renderer
  with `-u` straight into a log.
- `taskkill /PID` from Git Bash fails silently (the flag is rewritten as a path); the render
  I thought I had killed kept writing the same mp4 as its replacement, and the file decoded
  as garbage. `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 taskkill /PID <pid> /F`, then check the process list, then
  render again. Cost: one full re-render.
- The first "head hidden" bound was a geometry guess dressed as a witness. Look at the
  pixels at full resolution before any occlusion argument goes into a verdict.

## What to do differently next time

- A second phone from the side at hip height: chin depth, elbow asymmetry and true ROM in one.
- A 1 m stick held at the bar before the set; lock the exposure.
- Add DWPose (rtmlib, 133 keypoints with the jawline) as the third tracker; SAM 2 for the
  person and the cat in one pass (05-ml-tooling-survey.md).
