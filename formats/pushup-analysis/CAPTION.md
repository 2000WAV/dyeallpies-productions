Push Up Analysis by Fable

30 push-ups. Every one past parallel, every one locked out, 29 of 30 with a straight body. Judged by AI to the USMC standard, from one phone on the floor 🤖

The phone looks along my body from the head end, so nothing in the picture measures height directly. The AI closes the geometry with my own arm and trunk lengths from the pull-up videos, the hips' width, and the wrists on the floor. At the bottom of every rep my nose touches the floor and my shoulders sit 16 cm up, elbow at 69°. The colour on the body is a muscle model driven by physics: hand force, joint levers, force sharing, and a fatigue model that carries each rep into the next. Not a thermal camera, not EMG.

✅ 30/30 upper arms past parallel, 30/30 locked at the top
🟡 hips straight for 25 reps, then a 7 cm sag: the core gives before the arms
📉 push speed −26 % from the fastest rep; the descents go from 0.6 s to 2.5 s after rep 22
🔥 about 0.8 kcal a rep, measured on people in a lab, not guessed: 23 kcal for the set
🧾 the chest and triceps are co-dominant in the EMG literature; the model lands at 1.25 to 1, the papers say 0.6 to 1.2
