# Worked example — the first push-up set (IMG_6107.MOV, shot 2026-09-08, analysed 2026-09-09/10)

Full numbers: [`pushup/REPORT.md`](../../../../pushup/REPORT.md); the model:
[`pushup/PUSHUP-MODEL.md`](../../../../pushup/PUSHUP-MODEL.md); caption:
[`pushup/CAPTION.md`](../../../../pushup/CAPTION.md). This file records how the result was reached
and what went wrong on the way.

Dennis's brief (2026-09-09): the pull-up treatment for the push-up, with the pure blue-red effect and
the hook of the first pull-up reel (140k views); "a balance between science and viral: a full
academic basis, and on top research about viral techniques". Mid-way (2026-09-10) he sent a screen
recording of set #1 as the visual reference, asked for the legs in the heat map "even if
artificially", the map kept on while he stands up at the end, and to adapt the old code for the
look rather than approximate it.

## The clip

116.9 s iPhone 14 MOV, rotation tag 90 → 1080×1920, 30 fps, 3505 frames, 10-bit HLG. The phone
stands on its edge on the corridor floor at the head end (lens 13 cm up, looking 7.8° up along the
body); the hands flank it at the bottom corners of the frame, the feet are 2.2 m away. 30 reps in
106 s, no failed attempt; at the bottom of every rep the head comes within 40 cm of the lens and
hides the hips. The zip also held IMG_6108, which turned out to be pull-up set #3's source.

## Timeline of the session (two sittings, ≈9 h)

1. **Research first, five agents two at a time**, each one file into `references/pushup-science/`
   with abstracts, open-access texts and CSVs: EMG by variant and phase (37 studies; pec and
   triceps co-dominant, ant. deltoid 0.9 × pec, serratus 0.77 × pec), hand force and elbow moments
   (Eckel 2017 72 / 77 % BW; Donkers 1993 23 N·m = 56 % of MVIC; no shoulder moment exists), the
   USMC standard and norms (the .mil pages 403; two secondary compilations agree; Nakagata 2022
   0.77 kcal a rep measured), fatigue within a set (nothing push-up-specific exists), reel
   virality (12 recommendations; the preattentive case for a two-colour map; Crameri 2020).
2. **Master, trackers, matte** as the pull-up (libplacebo bt.2390; MediaPipe 9 min, YOLO 23,
   matte 40, 5 GB).
3. **The camera from the room**: the plank edges give the along-corridor vanishing point, the
   door jambs the vertical; f 1694 px (set #3 found 1950 on the same phone: the vertical VP's
   distance sets f and is weakly determined), pitch 7.8° up, roll −0.6°. The first fit had the
   world's up axis flipped (the wrists projected above the horizon).
4. **The geometry, five attempts.** (a) Two independent arm solvers (elbow circle + elbow ray):
   two branches per arm, straight arms degenerate. (b) One shared depth for both shoulders: better,
   still ill-conditioned at the tops. (c) The chain that stands: hips (width scale, no h_c) →
   shoulders (trunk sphere on their rays) → wrists (h_c) → elbow (circle, law of cosines). (d) A
   trunk-length closure for h_c was non-monotonic; the locked-arm closure at the tops is clean
   (13.0 cm; the lens height 12.5; the ankle row agrees). (e) The bottom: the first pass gated the
   shoulders out believing the head hid them and bridged with parabolas (the shoulder trajectory
   is unobserved for a second: the parabola knew nothing), then through the ears (the neck flexes:
   the offset flips sign); putting the shoulder dots at the head's depth gave the same 29–32 cm
   width as at the top, so they were the real deltoids beside the skull and the gate was wrong.
   The hips do vanish (their dots land on the skull).
5. **Rules that bit:** YOLO's ankles within a degree of the horizon (a degree is a metre); the
   parallel test's sign inverted (2/30 on a 30/30 set); lock-out at 150° failed five reps that
   MediaPipe and the frame call locked (2 cm of arm is 20° there) → 140°.
6. **The model** (`pushup_thermal.py`, from `pullup_thermal4`): with the athlete factor alone the
   pec sat at 1.0 whole rep and its pool at 80 % (failure by rep 15); a shoulder strength factor
   of 2.0 keeps the triceps / pec ratio at the top of the literature's band and the pool at 75 %;
   the anterior deltoid's arm 3.6 cm calibrated to the 0.9 ratio; levers and accelerations
   clipped (spikes of 486 / 977 N·m from a few frames outside the reps); the wrist lever 2 cm.
   The elbow side reproduced Donkers' 56 % untouched.
7. **The look, three passes.** The set #3 atlas as is (striations, six-hue scale) read purple and
   textured; set #1's LUT at 0.14 striation still read dark; Dennis's screen recording of set #1
   settled it, and its own painter (`pullup_heat.HeatPainter.paint`, commit cc01157) gave the
   recipe: luminance 0.78 + 0.26 × grey, a 25 % definition term, 0.96 opacity, no texture; the
   temperature term weighted up so the whole body drifts. The head gate had to follow the head's
   axis (the crown is two ear-widths from the ears at the bottom); the hands cut in a disc around
   the wrist (a half-plane took the whole leg when he stood up); the legs as static capsules.
8. **Plate:** Taman Negara (Argenberg, CC BY 4.0) above the corridor floor's far edge, the real
   floor kept. Commons "ground level" searches return nothing; the artist field of the search JSON
   truncates names (confirmed from the API's extmetadata).
9. Dashboard, report page (published as an Artifact), REPORT, CAPTION, this file, the mirror
   script; the bake, the previews, the exports, the gates.

## Headline numbers

30 reps · 30/30 past parallel (elbow 69° at the bottom, shoulder 16 cm up, nose at the floor) ·
30/30 locked (157° geometry, 167° MediaPipe) · 29/30 straight (hip sag −1 → −7 cm) · 37 cm of
shoulder travel · hands 38 cm apart, flare 40° · peak speed 0.84 m/s, −26 % vs the fastest ·
0.9 s down / 0.3 s bottom / 0.8 s up · 16.9 reps/min · hand force 72–77 % BW (measured), peak
1.32 BW · 0.77 kcal a rep (measured) · pec pool 75 %, triceps 43 % at the end (model).

## What to do differently next time

- A second phone at hip height, 3 m to the side (the hand position, the elbow through the
  bottom, the chest to the floor, the sag in its own plane).
- A tape on the hand spacing and a plank before the set.
- Ask for the set #1 reel file before matching a look; the screen recording was enough this time.
