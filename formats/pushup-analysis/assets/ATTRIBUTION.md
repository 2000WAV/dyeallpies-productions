A 1080×1200 crop of "Taman Negara, Malaysia, Tropical rainforest.jpg" by Vyacheslav Argenberg,
CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons, placed above the corridor floor's far edge (the real floor under
the hands stays; `pushup/work/build_plate.py`):
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Taman_Negara,_Malaysia,_Tropical_rainforest.jpg

Required credit line for the post: **"Background: Vyacheslav Argenberg / CC BY 4.0 (Wikimedia
Commons)."**

Two more of his photographs were downloaded as candidates and not used ("Khao Sok primary tropical
rainforest, southern Thailand", "Palawan, Tropical rainforest deep in Palawan wilderness"; both CC BY
4.0). All three originals are in `pushup/assets/commons/` with `CREDITS.json` (the artist name was
confirmed from the Commons API's extmetadata on 2026-09-10: the search field had truncated it).

Everything here is CC BY on purpose: a ShareAlike element would push the whole Reel into ShareAlike.
