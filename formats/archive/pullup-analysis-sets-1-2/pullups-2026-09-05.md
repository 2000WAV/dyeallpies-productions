# Worked example — the first pull-up set (IMG_6041.MOV, 2026-09-05)

Full numbers and coaching notes: [`REPORT.md`](REPORT.md);
caption: [`CAPTION.md`](CAPTION.md). This file records how the
result was reached and what went wrong on the way.

## The clip

55 s iPhone MOV, rotation tag 90 → 1080×1920 upright, 30 fps, 1655 frames. Phone on the
kitchen floor pointing up at a doorway bar. Dennis in shorts, facing the camera, legs
crossed behind; the knees leave the frame at the bottom of the hang. Static camera.

## Timeline of the session (≈75 min)

1. Probed the clip, contact sheet, found the old push-up venv (`.venv-pushup`: mediapipe
   0.10.35, ultralytics 8.4, torch CPU-only). Downloaded `pose_landmarker_heavy.task` and
   `yolov8m-pose.pt` into `tools/models/`.
2. Ran both trackers in the background in parallel: MediaPipe heavy 13 fps (199 s),
   YOLOv8m at imgsz 640 3.9 fps (417 s). MediaPipe found a pose on 1651/1655 frames.
3. **First analysis was wrong twice.** (a) Chin-based height signal + hands-up mask:
   the mask flickered at rep tops, the "longest run" started at 23.8 s and the first five
   reps were lost → 7 reps. (b) Fixed the window (merge gaps < 0.5 s) but the chin
   estimate said no rep reached the bar. A full-res crop at a rep top showed the whole
   head hidden behind the bar/lintel — MediaPipe was inventing a face at bar level with
   visibility 1.0. Switched the signal to the shoulder midpoint → 10 reps, YOLO agreed
   (r = 0.998 between the two shoulder tracks, tops within 0.3 s).
4. 2D elbow angle at the top read 8–19° (forearm pointing at the camera). Switched all
   joint angles to the 3D world landmarks → 57–85°, plausible.
5. Chin rule: shoulder-to-chin measured standing (13.1 cm) + 3 cm perspective allowance.
   YOLO eye confidence at the top (0.1–0.2 on reps 1–9, 0.54 on rep 10) was the ground
   truth that reps 1–9 cleared and rep 10 fell short.
6. Phase walker bug: walking *backward* from the next bottom exits immediately (v ≈ 0
   there), so every rest was 0.00 s. Walk forward from the top instead.
7. Rendered overlay (PIL + cv2, raw frames piped to NVENC, ~2.5 min), dashboard
   (matplotlib, dataviz palette), report, caption, this skill.

8. **Revision, same day.** Dennis pointed out he was standing until ~12 s. The arm-length
   and shoulder-line signals showed a standing plateau (4.8-9.8 s), a 1.8 s loading ramp
   (arms +9 %, shoulders -6.6 cm) and the loaded hang from 11.6 s. He also gave height
   and mass (188 cm, 79 kg): calibrating on the arm moved ROM from 49 to 56 cm and added
   work/power/kcal/effort. A segmentation pass produced the torso figure.

9. **Muscle heat overlay.** Dennis asked for a red-to-blue "contraction" heat map on his
   torso (partly to cover it). Built as an EMG-informed prior: Youdas 2010 %MVIC per
   muscle placed on the body from landmarks, phase factors from Dickie 2017, scaled by
   the measured pull speed, plus 25 % image contrast. First attempt (image contrast only)
   lit up the silhouette edge and was flat inside — the literature layer is what gives
   the map its structure. Abstracts fetched via PubMed E-utilities (PubMed and
   ScienceDirect block the page fetcher).

10. **Sharp outline.** The pose model's 256-px mask leaked onto the head and wall. Replaced
    by Robust Video Matting alpha × MediaPipe multiclass classes (`extract_matte.py`).
    First cut used body-skin only; the class flickered to "face" at the rep tops (no face
    in view) and called the tattoo "clothes", so the region became alpha minus *gated*
    head/clothes classes. Layout moved: chart to the bottom, full-width rep card,
    one-line legend, one elbow value (off-axis camera).
11. **Fatigue base, opacity, text fit.** Body-wide warm-up from measured velocity loss
    (Sánchez-Medina 2011) that never falls within the set; opacity 0.96; `fit()` for every
    burned-in line after two exports shipped with overflowing intro/summary lines; title
    "PULL UP ANALYSIS by Fable and DyeAllPies".
12. **Look pass and silhouette map.** Matte-driven background treatment, rim glow, grain,
    vignette (`pullup_look.py`); muscles assigned per pixel to the nearest segment with
    torso bands by distance-to-edge (`muscle_map_sil`), Dennis's idea.
13. **Jungle, flicker gate, verdicts.** CC BY plate from Wikimedia Commons with the real
    bar masked back in; `check_flicker.py` found four one-frame flashes = MediaPipe's
    dropped-pose frames (renderer now holds the last landmarks); rep cards say "chin above
    bar / at bar / N cm short" instead of a letter grade (4 / 5 / 1).

## Headline numbers

10 reps · 8 chin-at-bar (+1 marginal) · 10 lock-outs · ROM 56 cm (arm-anchored; 42-43
by the other calibrations) · 1.5 s up / 0.3 s hold / 1.4 s down · peak speed 0.93 to
0.50 m/s (-46 %) · 414 J and about 0.9 kcal per rep, about 9 kcal per set, 350 W best
mean power, about 850 W peak · last rep x1.7 effort, RIR 0 · hip sway 4 cm · elbow at
top L 65 / R 81 deg · knees ~70 deg, hip angle 143 deg at the top · grip 1.37 x
shoulders · 5.0 s standing on the bar + 1.8 s loading + 1.1 s dead hang before rep 1 ·
mean efficiency 92/100.

## What to do differently next time

- Film from further back or higher so the head never leaves the frame at the top; then
  the chin can be measured directly instead of inferred.
- A side-on second angle would make sway/kip and body line far more trustworthy.
- Skip the long dead hang if the set is a max-rep test.
