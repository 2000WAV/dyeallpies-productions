# Instagram caption — the hand puppet, neon on black (max 2000 characters, 5 hashtags, one paragraph per line)

I opened my hand in the dark and a neon marionette fell out 🪄

Five strings, one from each fingertip. It is not drawn in: it is simulated. A physics engine hangs a 15 cm figure from my real fingertips, tracked in 3D from the phone video, and the strings only ever pull, never push. When a finger drops, its string goes slack and that part of the puppet falls. When I spread my fingers, the arms open. The fall out of the fist is real free fall, the snap when the strings catch is real tension, the swing afterwards is a pendulum with the numbers a real marionette has.

💜 the glow is rendered on the graphics card: violet body, magenta and blue arms, red boots, each part lit from inside and blooming into the dark
🖐️ the hand is my real hand, cut out of the wall it was filmed against, and the puppet's light falls back on the fingers
🎯 the drop: 0.12 s of free fall before the first string catches, a snap at 24 times the puppet's weight
🕹️ mapping from real marionette stringing: middle finger to the head, index and ring to the shoulders, thumb and pinky to the hands

Everything is open, scripts and the research archive: github.com/DyeAllPies/dyeallpies-productions
