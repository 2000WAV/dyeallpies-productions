# Instagram caption — the hand puppet, neon on black (max 2000 characters, 5 hashtags, one paragraph per line)

A neon marionette hangs from my fingertips. The puppet is simulated, not drawn.

The hand was filmed with a phone in front of a plain wall and tracked in 3D. A physics engine hangs a 15 cm figure from the five tracked fingertips on five strings. The strings can only pull: when a finger drops, its string goes slack and that part of the puppet falls; when the fingers spread, the arms open. The fall out of the fist is free fall for 0.12 s until the first string catches, and the catch loads the string with 24 times the puppet's weight.

The stringing follows real marionette practice: middle finger to the head, index and ring finger to the shoulders, thumb and little finger to the hands.

The figure is rendered on the graphics card as neon: violet body, magenta and blue arms, red boots, each part lit from inside and glowing into the dark. The hand was cut out of the wall it was filmed against, and the puppet's light is reflected on the fingers and on the wall behind it. The ending pushes in on the puppet and fades to black, and the logo is rendered in the same neon look.

The scripts, the physics model and the research notes are public: github.com/DyeAllPies/dyeallpies-productions
