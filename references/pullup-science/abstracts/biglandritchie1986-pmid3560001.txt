1. J Physiol. 1986 Oct;379:451-9. doi: 10.1113/jphysiol.1986.sp016263.

Reflex origin for the slowing of motoneurone firing rates in fatigue of human 
voluntary contractions.

Bigland-Ritchie BR, Dawson NJ, Johansson RS, Lippold OC.

During fatigue from a sustained maximal voluntary contraction (m.v.c.) the mean 
motoneurone discharge rates decline. In the present experiments we found no 
recovery of firing rates after 3 min of rest if the fatigued muscle was kept 
ischaemic, but near full recovery 3 min after the blood supply was restored. 
Since 3 min is thus sufficient time for recovery of any central changes in 
excitability, the results support the hypothesis that, during fatigue, 
motoneurone firing rates may be regulated by a peripheral reflex originating in 
response to fatigue-induced changes within the muscle.
