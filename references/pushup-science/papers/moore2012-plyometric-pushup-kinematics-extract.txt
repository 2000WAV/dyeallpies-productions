NOTE ON "Peak Flight (m)" UNITS: defined in Methods as "the maximal vertical trunk position during
flight" -- i.e. an ABSOLUTE marker height (trunk marker height off the floor at flight apex), NOT a
displacement/jump-height delta. Do not read .76-.87 m as "how high the hands left the floor" (that
would be physically implausible for a push-up) -- it is the trunk marker's height above the floor,
consistent with an inclined prone-launch body position.

KEY NUMBER for 02-pushup-hand-force-and-joint-moments.md: peak landing vGRF on the (dominant) hand
during a plyometric push-up ranges ~0.69-0.78x bodyweight across box-drop heights 3.8-11.4 cm and
the clap variant, with the clap push-up producing significantly higher landing force (0.78 BW) than
all three box-drop heights despite similar peak-flight height to BD3 -- the paper's own stated
conclusion is that the CLAP push-up is the most mechanically intense of the four variants tested,
combining high landing force with the greatest elbow-flexion displacement (most eccentric control
required).
