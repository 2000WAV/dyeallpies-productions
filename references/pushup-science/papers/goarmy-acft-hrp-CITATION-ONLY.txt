=== Hand-Release Push-Up (HRP), as paraphrased from the official page ===
Starting position (on "GET SET"): prone, facing the start line, hands flat on the ground with index
fingers inside the outer edges of the shoulders, chest and the front of the hips and thighs on the
ground, toes touching the ground, feet together or up to a boot's width apart (feet stay within a
boot's width of each other throughout the event).

Up position (on "GO"): push the whole body up as a single unit to the front-leaning-rest position by
fully extending the elbows.

The distinguishing HRP mechanic (not required by the USMC standard push-up): lower all the way down
until the chest, hips AND thighs touch the ground; then lift BOTH hands completely off the floor,
extend the arms out to the sides, replace the hands, and press back up to full elbow lockout. This
hand-release-and-arm-extension step must happen at the bottom of EVERY repetition.

Body line: fairly straight line from head to heel throughout; front-leaning rest is the ONLY
authorized rest position (same rule as the USMC standard push-up).

=== Why this matters for the pipeline ===
The HRP's full chest-hips-thighs-to-ground contact plus mandatory hand-release is a substantially
different (and generally slower, more strictly bottom-out) movement than the USMC standard push-up,
which only requires upper-arms-parallel-to-ground at the bottom and never requires the hands to
leave the floor. Do NOT use USMC push-up rep-count norms as a stand-in for Army HRP rep counts, or
vice versa -- they are different exercises with different ROM and different pacing (the HRP's
release-and-reset step adds real time per rep that a standard push-up does not have). No numeric
AFT/ACFT HRP scoring table (rep counts by age/sex/points) was retrieved this session -- a future
session should try goarmy.com's own scoring-standards page and the official Army Training
Publications site (armypubs.army.mil, DA PAM 7-22) for a primary numeric table; this session
prioritized the kinetics/norms/fatigue scope in the task brief and did not chase the ACFT/AFT
scoring table itself down to a primary source, so it is marked NOT FOUND rather than estimated.
