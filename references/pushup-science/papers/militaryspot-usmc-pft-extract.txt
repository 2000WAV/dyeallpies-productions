=== USMC PFT push-up technique, as quoted by this secondary source ===
Starting position: "The Marine will assume the front-leaning rest position by placing the hands
where comfortable. The feet may be together or 12 inches apart (measured between the feet). When
viewed from the side, the body should form a generally straight line from the shoulders to the
ankles."

Depth requirement (bottom of the rep): "the entire body as a single unit until the upper arms are
at least parallel to the ground." Marines may not rest their chest on the deck except as required
to confirm proper arm positioning.

Top of the rep (lockout): "raising the entire body until the arms are fully extended."

No-rep / disqualifying-rep criteria: a repetition does NOT count if the Marine fails to (1) maintain
a generally straight body line, (2) lower until the upper arms reach parallel to the ground, or (3)
fully extend the arms at the top. Additionally, incorrectly-performed push-ups after the first 10
are not counted (i.e. some early-rep form leniency, then a stricter count).

Time limit: "a timed event to perform as many push-ups as possible in two minutes."

Rest rule: an ALTERED front-leaning rest position (sagging in the middle, back flexed) is the only
authorized way to pause without penalty. If the Marine rests on the ground, or lifts either hand or
foot off the ground, the performance is terminated (the set ends there).

=== Cross-check against the ACFT hand-release push-up (Army) wording, for contrast ===
See goarmy-acft-hrp-CITATION-ONLY.txt in this folder for the Army's hand-release variant, which adds
an explicit hand-release-and-arm-extension requirement at the bottom of every rep (not required by
the USMC standard push-up above) -- the two services' events are NOT the same movement despite both
being called "push-ups" in casual usage, and should not be scored on the same table.
