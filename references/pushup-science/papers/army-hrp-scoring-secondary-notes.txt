US Army hand-release push-up (HRP) scoring -- secondary sources only, collected 2026-09-09. No
official Army scoring table (DA PAM 7-22, armypubs.army.mil) was fetched this session; see
goarmy-acft-hrp-CITATION-ONLY.txt for the technique description (from the official goarmy.com page,
via WebFetch since direct curl was 403'd) and army-hrp-scoring-secondary-notes.txt (this file) for
the numeric scoring attempts, which disagree across sources for a documented, real reason: the
Army's own test and scoring scheme changed name and structure between 2020 and 2025.

=== Source 1: topendsports.com/testing/tests/push-up-hand-release.htm (fetched via WebFetch) ===
Describes the ORIGINAL Army Combat Fitness Test (ACFT), gender-NEUTRAL scoring, "as of 2020" per the
page's own framing:
  100 pts: 57+ reps
  90 pts:  47 reps
  80 pts:  37 reps
  70 pts:  27 reps
  60 pts:  10 reps (minimum passing)
  <60 pts: <10 reps (failing)
Applies to ages 17-80, no age-bracket or sex differentiation stated on this page.

=== Source 2: WebSearch summary (gymnasetips.com / militaryfitnesscalc.com / aftcalculator.com,
2026 pages) describing the CURRENT "AFT" (Army Fitness Test) ===
States that the ACFT was renamed/restructured into the AFT (Army Fitness Test) in June 2025, now
with SEX- and AGE-NORMED standards (a reversal from the original gender-neutral ACFT design), a
60-point-per-event minimum to pass, and a 350-point total-score structure across 5 events. Example
given: male soldier aged 22-26 needs 14 HRP reps for 60 points (pass) and 61 reps for the maximum
100 points; a female soldier in the same age band needs 11 reps for 50 points (not stated whether
50 or 60 is her passing threshold in the summary obtained). Combat-arms soldiers (regardless of sex)
are held to the male/sex-neutral column per this summary.

=== Why these two disagree, and how to use them ===
This is NOT a data-quality problem in the individual sources -- it reflects real policy history:
the Army launched the ACFT with gender-neutral scoring around 2019-2022, faced significant
controversy and multiple score-table revisions, and (per source 2) renamed/restructured the test to
the "AFT" with age/sex-normed scoring effective June 2025. Source 1's table (gender-neutral, 57 reps
for 100 pts) almost certainly reflects an EARLIER iteration of the standard; source 2's numbers
(sex/age-specific, only 61 reps for 100 pts in the cited age band) reflect the CURRENT (2025-2026)
AFT. Neither was independently verified against the official Army source this session (both are
third-party calculator/blog pages). If the pipeline needs a defensible current number, prefer
source 2's framing (age/sex-normed, current AFT) over source 1's (gender-neutral, likely superseded
ACFT), but flag the whole Army HRP scoring table as ESTIMATE-grade pending a primary-source fetch.
Do NOT average or otherwise combine the two tables' numbers -- they describe two different scoring
regimes, not two measurements of the same standard.
