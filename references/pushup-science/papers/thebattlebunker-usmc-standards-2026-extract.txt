NOTE: push-ups cap at 70 POINTS (not 100) even though the max-rep column (e.g. 87 reps at age
21-25) is labelled "Push-ups max (70 pts)" -- i.e. 87 reps earns the maximum 70 points a Marine can
get from the push-up event, NOT 100 points. This matters because the task brief describing this
sweep mis-stated the 21-25 max as "87 reps for 100 points" -- the 87-rep figure is correct, but it
maps to 70 points, not 100. Pull-ups (max 100 points) remain the only upper-body PFT event that can
reach the full 100-point ceiling; a Marine who elects push-ups over pull-ups gives up the
possibility of a perfect 300-point PFT even with a maxed push-up score. (Same fact already
documented from the pull-up side in references/pullup-science/03-norms-and-energy.md section 1.1:
"pull-ups (unlike push-ups, capped at 70 points) are the only upper-body event that can reach the
full 100.")

=== PLANK (replaced crunches as the 3rd PFT event) ===
Maximum (100 pts): hold time 3:45. Minimum passing (40 pts): hold time 1:10. (Single pass/fail-style
row on the page, not broken out by age in this table; age-adjusted hold-time targets given
separately for the 3-mile run, not reproduced here as out of scope for this push-up-focused file.)

=== PFT Class thresholds (male+female, all ages, 3-event total score) ===
1st Class: 235-300 (40pts min every event). 2nd Class: 200-234. 3rd Class: 150-199. Fail: <150 OR
<40 in any single event.
