=== THIS PAPER'S OWN NEW DATA (n=72 cis-female college students, 18-24y, mean age 20.38) ===
Mean standard push-up reps: 9 (SD 8.87). Mean modified push-up reps: 17.5 (SD 11.76). 8/72 (11.1%)
could not complete even 1 standard rep; 7/72 completed >20 standard reps.
Resistance-trainers vs non-trainers: standard 12 (SD 10.37) vs 5.8 (SD 4.33), p=.001; modified 20
(SD 13.76) vs 14.41 (SD 7.79), p=.02.
Correlation modified-vs-standard reps: r=0.85, r^2=0.72 (p<.001). Regression:
  standard_reps = -2.217 + 0.64 x modified_reps
Applied to the existing modified-push-up category cut-points to derive a NEW predicted
standard-push-up scale for college-age women (rounded):
  Excellent:   >=18
  Very Good:   12-17
  Good:        8-11
  Fair:        5-7
  Needs Improvement: <=4
Compared against Mozumdar et al. (2010)'s Revised Push-up percentiles: this study's predicted scale
and Mozumdar's 80th/60th/40th percentile cut-points (16/10/6, respectively for the same 3
categories) are broadly consistent -- closer to Mozumdar's numbers than to the (more demanding)
Baumgartner et al. (2004) Revised Push-up percentiles or the FitnessGram Healthy Zone (which
expects 18-35 reps at age 17 in the standard position -- notably higher than this study's
"Excellent" threshold of 18 for college women).
