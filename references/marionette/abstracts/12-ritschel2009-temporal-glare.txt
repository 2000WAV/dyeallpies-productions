Abstract (verbatim from the DTU Orbit record): glare from light scattered in the eye
has previously been modelled only as a STATIC pattern; this paper argues the TEMPORAL
(fluctuating-in-time) properties of glare are themselves a strong perceptual cue, and
presents a real-time GPU model of dynamic ocular glare for tone-mapping HDR imagery to
LDR displays. A psychophysical study validated that the dynamic version increases
perceived brightness of the light source and is often judged more attractive than a
static glare rendering, scene-dependently.

Used in item 12 only for the qualitative point that a glare/bloom halo which subtly
breathes/flickers over time reads as MORE convincing and brighter than a static one --
no numeric halo-width or ratio figure is available from this abstract; the numeric
