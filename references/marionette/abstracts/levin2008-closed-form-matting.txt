Levin, A., Lischinski, D., and Weiss, Y. (2008). A Closed-Form Solution to Natural
Image Matting. IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence,
30(2), 228-242. (Conference version: CVPR 2006.)

STATUS: full text saved (author mirror, MIT CSAIL), text-extracted; see
papers/levin2008-closed-form-matting.pdf/.txt.

SUMMARY: Derives the "matting Laplacian": under a local color-line assumption (in
a small window, foreground and background colors each lie near a line in RGB
space), the alpha matte that is linear in the image colors within each window can
be solved for in closed form, in the whole image at once, given only a sparse
trimap or a few scribbles -- no explicit foreground/background color sampling
step. The matting Laplacian is a sparse matrix built purely from local image
statistics (per-window color covariance) and is later reused, as an image prior,
in guided filtering, dehazing, colorization, and other edge-aware tasks.

RELEVANCE: the classical (pre-deep-learning) high-quality matting method for
fine, wispy edges (hair, fur -- here, finger silhouettes and any stray hair on
the hand) from a trimap; contrasted in 05-camera-light-compositing.md with the
guided filter (a fast local-linear approximation to the same color-line idea) and
with learned mattes (RVM/MODNet/ViTMatte) that skip the trimap entirely.
