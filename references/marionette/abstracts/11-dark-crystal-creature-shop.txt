SUMMARY: production background on the Creature Shop's puppet-building
process (both the 1982 film and the 2019 series): characters are sculpted
in clay, molded, and built as foam-latex-skinned puppets over an armature;
the 2019 series alone involved more than 170 puppets and a team of over 125
artists covering faces, bodies, costumes, hair and accessories -- i.e. a
convincing puppet creature is a MATERIALS and CRAFT problem (skin texture,
weight, articulation) as much as a performance one.

KEY NUMBERS: 170+ puppets, 125+ artists (2019 series).

RELEVANCE: item 11 section 6 -- contrasts with the simplified-figure
precedent (Trnka) by showing the OTHER end of the puppet-realism spectrum:
Henson's creatures invest heavily in surface material fidelity (skin,
texture, hair) specifically because their characters ARE meant to read as
believable, near-photoreal creatures, not stylized figures -- useful
calibration for how much material investment (item 4) would be needed if
the marionette's look is later pushed toward that end rather than staying
a stylized painted-wood/plastic figure.
