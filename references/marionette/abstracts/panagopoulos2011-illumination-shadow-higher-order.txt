Panagopoulos, A., Samaras, D., and Paragios, N. (2011). Illumination Estimation and
Cast Shadow Detection through a Higher-Order Graphical Model. CVPR 2011.

STATUS: full text saved (Stony Brook CVL mirror), text-extracted; see
papers/panagopoulos2011-illumination-shadow-higher-order.pdf/.txt.

SUMMARY: Follow-up to the 2009 mixture-model paper. Replaces the pairwise MRF with
a higher-order MRF that combines low-level shadow evidence (intensity ratios
across the shadow boundary) with high-level scene priors, recovering both the
cast-shadow mask and a refined illumination-environment estimate jointly from one
image and coarse geometry.

RELEVANCE: same family as Panagopoulos 2009 -- background literature for
single-image illumination-from-shadow estimation, cited alongside Sato 2003 and
Lalonde 2009/2012 in the "estimating the light from a shadow on a wall" section.
