SUMMARY: standard thin-lens depth-of-field formulas -- hyperfocal distance
H = f^2/(N*c) + f, near/far limits Dn = H*s/(H+(s-f)), Df = H*s/(H-(s-f))
for a given focus distance s, focal length f, f-number N and circle-of-
confusion diameter c. The circle-of-confusion page documents the common
"diagonal/1500" convention for choosing c from the sensor's diagonal size
(a somewhat conservative choice tuned for typical viewing/print
conditions), alongside the alternative of tying c directly to pixel pitch
(e.g. c = 2 pixels) for a pixel-peeping/on-screen-viewing criterion.
