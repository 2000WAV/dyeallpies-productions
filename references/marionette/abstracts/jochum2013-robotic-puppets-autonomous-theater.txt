research programme, describing the string-driven "Prince Wen" / stepper-motor
marionette stand, the mixed dynamic-kinematic model of Johnson & Murphey (2007),
motion-primitive libraries, and audience/critical reception of robot-performed
puppet theater. Contains the group's full reference list (used in this project to
confirm the correct venue and co-authorship of Johnson & Murphey 2007 and Martin
et al. 2011).
