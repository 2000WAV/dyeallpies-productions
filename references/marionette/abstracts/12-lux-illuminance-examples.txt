Values used in item 12 as an anchor for "typical indoor/window light" against which a
small emissive figure's own contribution can be compared:
- 50 lux: family living room lighting (Australia, 1998 source cited on the page).
- 320-500 lux: office lighting.
- 400 lux: sunrise or sunset on a clear day (direct, outdoor).
- 1000 lux: overcast daylight; also typical TV-studio lighting level.

No number on this page is specific to "light received a metre back from a window on an
overcast day," which is the actual geometry of item 05's hand-lighting setup; the
100-1000 lux band above (indoor room lighting through to overcast-day light level) is
used in item 12 as a plausible bracket for the ambient/window fill on the hand, NOT as
a measurement of this specific shot (item 05's own archive has no photometer reading of
the plate, only a geometric light-DIRECTION solve from the hand's cast shadow -- see
item 05 and item 11 section 2.2). Flagged accordingly wherever used.
