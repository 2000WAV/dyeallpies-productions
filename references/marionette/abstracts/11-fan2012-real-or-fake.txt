SUMMARY (from secondary description only): an early forced-choice study of
whether ordinary viewers can tell CG face renders from photographs; found
that observers generally CAN tell them apart above chance, and that later
work in the same group (Fan et al.'s subsequent ACM TAP 2014 "Human
Perception of Visual Realism for Photo and Computer-Generated Face Images")
identified the EYES as the single most influential region for the
real/fake judgment.
