SUMMARY: VFX-trade-press account of ShadowMachine's stop-motion puppet
fabrication for del Toro's Pinocchio (2022). Two puppet techniques used
side by side: mechanical (Geppetto -- a puppet with an internal moving
mechanism, animated by adjusting the mechanism between frames) and
REPLACEMENT (Pinocchio himself -- since the character is meant to look
literally carved from wood, each facial expression/mouth shape is a
separate, individually fabricated piece swapped between frames, some
metal-3D-printed). More than 150 puppets were built in total across the
production (935 shooting days).

KEY NUMBERS: 150+ puppets; 935 days of filming.

RELEVANCE: item 11 section 6 -- the most directly relevant precedent for
"a puppet meant to visibly read as CARVED WOOD": del Toro's production
chose to fabricate discrete wood-textured pieces (replacement technique)
rather than trying to deform a single continuous material, because a
believable "wooden" read depends on the surface keeping wood's actual
visual behaviour (grain, hard edges at joints, no soft deformation) rather
than smoothly blending like flesh -- directly relevant if the marionette's
graphics-plan follow-up (HANDOFF.md item 3) pursues a "matte painted wood"
material with visible joints/seams at each capsule-to-capsule connection,
rather than a smooth continuous body.
