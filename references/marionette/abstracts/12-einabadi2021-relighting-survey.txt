Abstract (verbatim): surveys deep-neural approaches to scene relighting and
illumination estimation for inserting virtual objects into real scenes (mixed
reality), organised into three categories -- scene illumination estimation, relighting
via reflectance-aware scene-specific representations, and relighting as image-to-image
translation -- with a discussion of characteristics/future trends per category and an
overview of public datasets.
