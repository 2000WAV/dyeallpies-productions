Ke, Z., Sun, J., Li, K., Yan, Q., and Lau, R. W. H. (2020/2022). MODNet: Real-Time
Trimap-Free Portrait Matting via Objective Decomposition. AAAI 2022
(arXiv:2011.11961).

STATUS: full text saved (arXiv), text-extracted; see
papers/ke2020-modnet.pdf/.txt.

SUMMARY: Decomposes portrait matting into three easier sub-objectives solved by
three interdependent branches of one lightweight network: semantic estimation
(coarse foreground/background), boundary/detail estimation (a narrow band around
the boundary), and final alpha fusion -- each supervised by an explicit
constraint derived from the ground-truth alpha, rather than end-to-end alpha
regression alone. Trimap-free (single RGB image in, alpha out) and fast enough
for real-time use (67 fps on a 1080 Ti); extended with a non-learned flicker-
reduction trick for video.

RELEVANCE: a second learned-matte candidate for the hand; MODNet's "detail
branch" is specifically tuned for clean matting of thin structures near
boundaries (fingers, finger gaps) against a plain background, which is the exact
geometry of the shot (down-pointing fingers against a uniform off-white wall).
