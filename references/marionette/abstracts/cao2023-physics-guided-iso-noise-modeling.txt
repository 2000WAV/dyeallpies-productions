Cao, Y., et al. (2023). Physics-Guided ISO-Dependent Sensor Noise Modeling for
Extreme Low-Light Photography. CVPR 2023.

STATUS: full text saved (CVF open access); see
papers/cao2023-physics-guided-iso-noise-modeling.pdf.

SUMMARY: Models raw sensor noise as a physically-motivated mixture, photon shot
noise, read noise, banding or row noise, and quantization, whose parameters vary
with ISO and are calibrated per color channel (per Bayer or CFA position) rather
than as a single signal-independent Gaussian; shows this heteroscedastic,
per-channel, per-ISO model matches real extreme-low-light sensor noise far better
than a flat noise assumption, improving denoising and synthetic-noise generation
trained on it.

RELEVANCE: the grain-matching step, matching the plate's per-channel noise level
when compositing the CG marionette, should add noise with this kind of
signal-dependent, per-channel character rather than flat Gaussian grain. Even
though this shot is well-lit rather than extreme low-light, the same qualitative
point holds: real sensor noise variance grows with signal and gain and differs
slightly per color channel, which is what makes flat added grain look subtly
wrong on close inspection.
