SUMMARY: a practical, non-academic construction manual for wooden string
marionettes of traditional (child/stage) scale. Directly relevant sections
(located by full-text search):
- Knee and ankle joints are made from a strip of LEATHER, tacked/nailed so as "to
  allow for a bend" -- i.e. a leather-strap hinge is explicitly a single-axis
  (1-DOF), unstiffened, friction-only joint with no spring return, matching the
  "r-joint" (1-DOF revolute) category in Chen/Tay/Xing/Yeo's more formal
  classification.
- Shoulder and hip pieces are joined to the torso by a "muslin" cloth strip or "the
  ribbed top of a stocking," i.e. a flexible fabric hinge rather than a rigid pin --
  again a soft, low-stiffness, high-compliance joint.
- Screw-eyes are used at the shoulder, hip, knee, and head purely as string
  attachment/pass-through points, not as the joints themselves.
- No angular range-of-motion or stiffness numbers are given anywhere (this is a
  hobbyist craft manual, not an engineering paper) -- recorded as NOT FOUND for any
  quantitative joint data; only the qualitative construction (leather = hinge,
  cloth = flexible universal joint) is usable.
