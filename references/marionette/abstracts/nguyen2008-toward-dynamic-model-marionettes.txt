ABSTRACT (as printed):
Robotic marionettes are those mechatronic systems in which strings that control
motion of a puppet are driven by a puppeteer platform including motor-pulley
modules. Systematic control of robotic marionettes is now still a tough problem,
which several research groups have been attempting to solve. In this paper, we
present dynamic models of the robotic marionette developed from the Lagrange
equation. Feed-forward control and feedback control are studied based on the
dynamic models with the illustration of block diagrams. A process of identifying the
parameters appearing in the dynamic models is presented in detail. The modeling
provides better insight of the system, and builds a mathematical platform to
research the control and design of robotic marionette systems.

RELEVANCE: this is the Nanyang Technological University "ROMS" (Robotic Marionette
System) group's own dynamic-modeling paper; see also
icdst-marionette-robotic-manipulation.txt (Chen, Tay, Xing & Yeo, "Marionette: From
Traditional Manipulation to Robotic Manipulation") for the ROMS-I/II/III hardware
specification table used for the size/mass/joint-count numbers in
data/02-dynamics-numbers.csv.
