SUMMARY: a widely-used, open-source (Apache-2.0 project) physically-based
rendering material reference. Documents the standard metallic/roughness
PBR parameterization: `roughness` in [0,1] controls perceived surface
smoothness (0=mirror-glossy, 1=fully rough/matte), for both dielectric
(non-metal, e.g. plastic/paint/wood-with-clearcoat) and conductor (metal)
surfaces, plus a dedicated CLEAR COAT layer model explicitly intended for
"car paint, soda cans, lacquered wood, and coated metals" -- i.e. Filament's
own documentation explicitly names lacquered wood as the canonical
clear-coat use case: a rough/matte or textured base layer (the wood) plus a
thin, separate, near-perfectly-smooth (very low roughness) clear coat on
top, each with its own roughness value, rather than one single roughness
number for the whole surface.
