Lin, S., Yang, L., Saleemi, I., and Sengupta, S. (2021/2022). Robust High-
Resolution Video Matting with Temporal Guidance. WACV 2022 (arXiv:2108.11515).

SUMMARY: "Robust Video Matting" (RVM). A recurrent (ConvGRU-based) architecture
that estimates a human's alpha matte and foreground frame-by-frame from ordinary
video WITHOUT a green screen, trimap, or known background, exploiting temporal
information across frames for both speed and stability (reduces flicker vs
independent per-frame matting). Trained on a mix of matting datasets (with known
alpha) and unlabeled real videos (via an adversarial/segmentation consistency
loss) to generalize to in-the-wild footage. Reports 4K 76 fps / HD 104 fps on a
GTX 1080 Ti.

RELEVANCE: a candidate learned matte for pulling the hand off the plain wall
directly from the phone video, frame-consistent across the 9 s / 270-frame clip
without per-frame trimaps; the recurrent temporal state is specifically useful
here because the hand is nearly static (small handheld sway) so flicker in a
per-frame matte would be very visible.
