WHAT WAS OBTAINED INSTEAD: Dempster's actual numbers, as reprinted verbatim in
Winter, D.A., Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement, Table 4.1
("Anthropometric Data"), saved from a course PDF at
papers/winter-anthro-table-illinois-me481.pdf (text extract in the .txt of the same
name). Winter's table cites each row's ultimate source as Dempster via Miller &
Nelson (1973) or Dempster via Plagenhoef (1971) -- coded M, P, L, C in the table's
footnote. It gives, per segment: segment weight / total body weight; center-of-mass
position as a fraction of segment length from the proximal and distal ends; radius of
gyration about the segment's own CG, and about the proximal and distal ends, each as
a fraction of segment length; and segment density. These are the numbers transcribed
into data/02-segment-parameters.csv as the "Dempster (via Winter Table 4.1)" rows,
used as a second, independent check against de Leva (1996) for the same segments.

Also NOT FOUND: an open numeric table for Drillis, R. and Contini, R. (1966), Body
Segment Parameters, Report 1166-03, NYU School of Engineering and Science (the
usual source for segment LENGTH as a fraction of stature, reproduced only as a
figure -- not extractable as text -- in Winter's book). As a substitute with real
